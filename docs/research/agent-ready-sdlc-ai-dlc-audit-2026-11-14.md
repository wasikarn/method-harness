**Date:** 2026-11-14

**Verdict:** The graphic's right column is a fair compression of Anthropic's playbook, with small slips (its "evals" label for the Test artifact, "last commit" for "the prior stage's committed artifact"). Its left column describes AI-DLC as it was in 2025: "open-source rules" and "Operations: placeholder in v1" are out of date, since AI-DLC is now an engine with a CLI, hooks, sensors and a 134-event audit trail, and its profiles page calls Operation a placeholder for `classic` only, while the scopes page shows Operation skipped in `classic`, `mvp` and `poc` and run as 7 conditional stages in others. For this repo, nothing the graphic carries is both missing and worth building now. The production gate and "CLAUDE.md plus hooks, not a wiki" are already here. The two real gaps (a committed per-change intent file, and a record of plan approvals) have no measured demand, the premise ("planning and review are the limit") is unproven in this repo's own data, and a gate-shaped version of either is frozen by ADR 0005. Recommendation: build nothing native; defer the cheap advisory ideas behind a bare-Claude pilot; if the operator wants to learn from AI-DLC, trial it on a throwaway project outside mh.

## Deliberately not shipped

- **A native copy of AI-DLC's profiles or stages (idea c)** — `docs/METHODOLOGY.md` Rule 1 already sizes work, there is no measured demand, and `docs/reference/composer-not-creator.md:36-38` builds a native surface only after a bare-Claude pilot shows headroom; the premise evidence cuts the other way (`docs/adr/0005-gate-scope-agent-mistakes-not-disguised-commands.md:15-23`). Doctrine anchor: composer-not-creator and YAGNI. Labeled: **declined on evidence**.
- **A committed `intent.md`/`spec.md`/`plan.md` convention (idea a) and question files (idea b)** — `docs/reference/mattpocock-integration-map.md:25` lists `implement`, `to-spec` and `to-tickets` as the spec-to-ship chain where "mh names no step of it", and Rule 3 plus `agents/requirement-analyst.md` already cover asking first. Cheap, advisory and reversible, so worth a bare-Claude pilot (build only if bare Claude scores 0.85 or lower on a blind case). Doctrine anchor: composer-not-creator § Headroom pilot. Labeled: **deferred**.
- **A record of plan approvals (idea d), in particular a presence receipt like AI-DLC's `HUMAN_TURN`** — a gate-shaped change to `hooks/gates/` needs one of ADR 0005's four allowed reasons (`docs/adr/0005-gate-scope-agent-mistakes-not-disguised-commands.md:54-67`), and none applies without a real miss where an agent claimed an approval nobody gave. Merge approval is already recorded by the owner comment. Doctrine anchor: ADR 0005, maker≠checker. Labeled: **deferred**.
- **Installing AI-DLC into mh or a host project** — a third-party installer (`curl ... | sh`) that writes broad allow rules and `*`-matcher hooks into the project settings; coexistence with mh's gates is untested. If the operator wants the experience, the first trial belongs on a throwaway project, named and approved by the operator (an install is a T3 action). Doctrine anchor: Rule 1 (one-way door, blast radius). Labeled: **deferred**.
- **Named human roles per stage (idea h)** — one operator; the only role in the repo is the OWNER check at `scripts/merge-pr.sh:288-305`. Doctrine anchor: YAGNI. Labeled: **premise dead**.
